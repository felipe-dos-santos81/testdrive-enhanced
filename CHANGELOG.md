# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Mouse steering is now by pointer position: left of the window centre turns left, right of it turns
  right, the middle band goes straight, and a parked pointer holds the turn. Over the button strip or
  with the pointer outside the window the car goes straight.
- New strip cells: QUIT (hold about half a second to end the drive; a tap does nothing) and GBOX (tap
  to show or hide the gear box, the keyboard `d`).
- Strip cells highlight under the pointer and brighter while held; a held cell stays lit if the
  pointer slides off it.

## v1.0.1

- Smoother horizon: the valley floor and the foot of the mountains now meet at the same haze level, so the
  bright mist band between them is gone. The mist builds up gradually towards the horizon and thins out
  evenly a few rows up the mountains.

## v0.1.0

- First release: enhanced renderer on the faithful Test Drive engine, higher-resolution road view, sprite
  detail levels.
